    How pairings are scored

    Each family's regular style is measured from its outlines: x-height against cap height, letter width, stem weight, stroke contrast and its angle, how open letters like c are, and whether it has serifs.

    A pair scores well when its proportions agree, so both faces share a rhythm, and its style differs by about as much as the Contrast slider asks. The body face must hold up in paragraphs. Near-identical faces of the same genre are penalised; designed families like IBM Plex Sans and Serif get a bonus. Discovery moves results between well-known and lesser-known families.
